Investment Approach & Experience

Grounded in real-world ownership and analysis.

Watershed Equity is led by its principal, Shelby Rognstad.

His experience includes acquiring and repositioning residential and commercial properties, developing financial models, managing assets, and executing projects designed to strengthen cash flow and long-term value.

Through Watershed Equity, he has evaluated and participated in private real estate and alternative investment placements, gaining additional experience in sponsor evaluation, investment underwriting, due diligence, and risk assessment.

Watershed Equity has also undertaken strategic consulting for Intech, a bulk internet service provider, identifying a new business development opportunity and developing a financial modeling framework to demonstrate the potential return on investment and build a compelling business case for prospective customers.

His approach emphasizes understanding the underlying economics, questioning assumptions, assessing downside exposure, and identifying practical strategies to mitigate risk and improve performance.

Services

Watershed Equity provides financial analysis, strategic planning, and project-based services tailored to the needs of property owners, investors, and organizations.

Watershed Equity / Services
01

Asset Strategy & Optimization

Evaluate property performance, identify opportunities to strengthen cash flow, assess capital improvements, and develop strategies for repositioning or improving the long-term value of real estate assets.

02

Financial Analysis & Strategic Evaluation

Develop financial models, evaluate project economics, assess alternative scenarios, and examine the assumptions and risks underlying significant business and investment decisions.

03

Development & Redevelopment Strategy

Assess development and adaptive reuse opportunities, evaluate feasibility, establish project priorities, and help owners translate creative concepts into financially grounded, actionable plans. Owner's representation, project oversight, and implementation support are also available for select engagements.

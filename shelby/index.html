Background

My experience spans nearly three decades of real estate ownership, investment, and development, along with founding and operating businesses. I've evaluated investments, financed and improved properties, managed complex projects, and built organizations. My background also includes 15 years of public service, including eight years as mayor of Sandpoint, Idaho, where I helped advance initiatives involving economic development, infrastructure, land use planning, and public-private collaboration.

I bring an entrepreneurial and owner's perspective to strategic challenges—combining financial discipline, creative thinking, and practical execution to navigate competing interests and create lasting value.

About

About Me

I've always been drawn to opportunities that combine strategic thinking, creativity, and entrepreneurial initiative. I enjoy recognizing potential, exploring possibilities, and bringing together people and resources to turn promising ideas into tangible results.

My experience has shaped a practical approach to complex challenges: establish a clear objective, understand the economics, consider different perspectives, and develop a thoughtful strategy for moving forward.

I believe strong leadership combines ambitious thinking with intellectual humility—the confidence to pursue worthwhile ideas and the judgment to recognize when others have better answers.

Today, I'm interested in contributing my experience and perspective to organizations pursuing thoughtful investment, creative development, and meaningful growth.

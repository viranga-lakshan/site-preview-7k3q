Downtown Sandpoint Commercial Redevelopment

Acquired a vacant, distressed commercial property in 2013, recognizing the long-term potential of its prime downtown location despite significant physical deterioration and negligible income. Developed and executed a phased repositioning strategy, renovating existing storefronts, converting underutilized space into an additional leasable commercial unit, and improving the building's appearance and functionality. Prioritized capital improvements and tenant recruitment to progressively restore occupancy and strengthen cash flow. Today, the property is a stabilized, income-producing asset with three commercial units and long-term tenants, including a prominent restaurant that has become one of downtown Sandpoint's busiest dining destinations over the past decade.

02

Common Knowledge | Bookstore, Café & Cultural Venue

Acquired an established downtown Sandpoint bookstore and property in 2005, reimagining and rebranding it as Common Knowledge—a distinctive mixed-use destination combining books, dining, entertainment, and community gathering spaces. Conceived and directed an extensive redevelopment that introduced a commercial kitchen and café, additional residential accommodations, and a landscaped outdoor garden and event space, while transforming the building's interior and exterior into an inviting, design-rich environment. Developed and operated a health-focused restaurant that expanded into evening dining and live entertainment, establishing Common Knowledge as a vibrant cultural gathering place. As owner-operator, oversaw business development, operations, and a multidisciplinary team of more than 20 employees.

03

Downtown Sandpoint Residential Development

Acquired an underperforming residential rental property near downtown Sandpoint, recognizing the long-term potential of its highly walkable location. Initially maintained the existing structure as an income-producing rental before undertaking a complete redevelopment when market conditions supported new construction. Conceived the architectural design for a distinctive modern residence grounded in the local architectural vernacular, working with a drafter to develop construction plans and overseeing the ground-up build as owner-builder. Subsequently adapted the property into two independent rental units, creating a flexible hospitality-oriented asset that capitalizes on its proximity to downtown amenities and recreational destinations.

04

James Russell Sports Complex

Helped guide a $8 million municipal recreation investment from initial concept through successful completion, contributing to site selection, capital raising, facility programming, design, stakeholder engagement, and budget oversight. Evaluated alternative locations, helped shape facility features to maximize functionality and community value, and navigated significant public opposition through stakeholder engagement and negotiated compromises. Worked with municipal leadership to maintain financial discipline, contributing to the project's completion on schedule and within its approved budget.

Municipal Fiber Infrastructure

Helped advance a municipal fiber backbone designed to expand high-speed internet access and encourage competition among private service providers. Supported a consultant-developed infrastructure strategy that leveraged public investment to reduce barriers to private-sector participation. The initiative progressed through budgeting and construction over approximately three years, establishing infrastructure to support expanded broadband availability and provider competition city-wide.

06

Watershed Crest Trail Network

Originated the vision for a destination recreational trail network on publicly owned watershed lands and built support among nonprofit organizations, municipal departments, government agencies, private landowners, and elected leaders. Collaborated on legislation and master plans establishing a framework for trail development and maintenance through a public-private-nonprofit partnership, helping secure institutional approval and funding. Implementation of this long-term regional recreation initiative remains ongoing.
